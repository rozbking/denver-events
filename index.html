Astronomy Night — Denver Astronomical Society September through March: 7:30pm start (April through August it is 8:30pm) · Chamberlin Observatory, Observatory Park · Each night opens with a multimedia astronomy talk by a DAS lecturer, pitched for all ages. Clear skies mean telescopic views of the moon, stars, star clusters, planets, galaxies and nebulae through Chamberlin's historic 28-foot, 20-inch-aperture Alvan Clark-Saegmuller refractor. Cloudy nights become an observatory tour instead: the clocks, the meridian telescope, the library, the wall displays and an animated sky map for the month, with DAS volunteers on hand for questions. How to actually get tickets Reservations at denverastro.org/reservations · Most tickets unlock automatically 60 days before each event at 12am, so check the page daily and early. A small number are held back each month and released on the 1st of the event month, usually between 6 and 7am.
