Denver After Dark — Capitol Hill haunted walking tour Fridays and Saturdays May through November; Tuesday through Saturday in October · Departures: Tue–Wed 7pm · Thu 8pm · Fri & Sat 7:00, 7:30 or 8:00pm · 2 to 2.5 hours, 1.7 miles, easy and flat with plenty of stops · Meet at the SE corner of W 13th Ave and N Gilpin St, ends at the Molly Brown House, 1340 Pennsylvania St · A guided night walk through Capitol Hill built on a decade of research: documented haunted history and paranormal legend rather than actors and fog machines. The route takes in the Treat-Rogers Mansion, Stoiberhof, a burial ground turned park, and Cheesman Park, built over a former city cemetery whose bodies were never fully removed. Two stops have been on national television, the Croke-Patterson Mansion on Portals to Hell and the Peabody-Whitehead Mansion on Ghost Adventures, and the tour visits the real Denver locations behind both Poltergeist and The Changeling. Fall dates sell out every year; booking before Oct 1 gets the lowest price.

Astronomy Night — Denver Astronomical Society September through March: 7:30pm start (April through August it is 8:30pm) · Chamberlin Observatory, Observatory Park · Each night opens with a multimedia astronomy talk by a DAS lecturer, pitched for all ages. Clear skies mean telescopic views of the moon, stars, star clusters, planets, galaxies and nebulae through Chamberlin's historic 28-foot, 20-inch-aperture Alvan Clark-Saegmuller refractor. Cloudy nights become an observatory tour instead: the clocks, the meridian telescope, the library, the wall displays and an animated sky map for the month, with DAS volunteers on hand for questions. How to actually get tickets Reservations at denverastro.org/reservations · Most tickets unlock automatically 60 days before each event at 12am, so check the page daily and early. A small number are held back each month and released on the 1st of the event month, usually between 6 and 7am. Watch for Saturdays: those are normally private group bookings, but any Saturday still open on the 1st gets added as an extra Astronomy Night and released that morning. Occasionally a date sells out early to a special booking, so a release can be thin or skipped. Tickets are non-transferable except through the DAS reservations team, and at least one attendee's name must be on the official guest list to get in, so ignore anyone reselling.
